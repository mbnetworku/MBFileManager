## 2.9.0.034
- Music: the player now plays from all the songs on your phone (no folder limit), starting with the song you tap.
- Music player on car radios: the close (X) button moved to the right side so it is not covered.
## 2.9.0.033
- Music player on car radios: bigger, white text for the song title, folder, time and song list, and a clearer equalizer (fewer, thicker bars in white and blue).
## 2.9.0.032
- New look for the music player: the background takes the colour of the song's cover and changes smoothly between songs; liquid-glass panels; a white play button; buttons that press in and spring back; the title fades in on each song. Same controls and layout.
## 2.9.0.031
- Music player in landscape (car radios): song list across the top, equalizer and playback buttons below. Tap the equalizer to change its look (bars, lines or dots).
## 2.9.0.030
- New: swipe left / right on the mini player bar or on the full player to change song.
- Changed: the mini player bar comes back when you press play again after hiding it.
## 2.9.0.029
- New: a progress bar for copy, move and delete. You can hide it with its X; it stays in the notification with a progress bar, and a short "Done" notice appears when it finishes.
- Fixed: the music player in landscape (cover and controls now sit side by side).
## 2.9.0.028
- Fixed: Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox sign-in. It now works through the official cloud apps on your phone (normal sign-in, personal and Microsoft 365 accounts) - no keys needed.
## 2.9.0.027
- Cloud: normal sign-in for Google Drive, OneDrive (personal and Microsoft 365) and Dropbox - no keys to type.
- New: after you edit a file from a server or cloud account, the app offers to save it back.
## 2.9.0.026
- New: a short "What's new" message now appears after each update, and also in the update-available window before you update.
## 2.9.0.025

- New: Move to folder... and Copy to folder... with a folder picker.

- New: a short "What's new" message after every update.
